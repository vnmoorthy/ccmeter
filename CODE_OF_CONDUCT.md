# Code of Conduct

ccmeter follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, focus on the work. Maintainers may moderate or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

For reports, open a private issue or email the maintainers (address in repo profile).
